## Thread reply 1

Every finding includes:

- URL or location
- action taken
- expected vs. actual result
- user impact
- severity
- recommendation
- confidence and limitations

## Thread reply 2

It is read-only by default. It will not purchase, delete, publish, send, or submit anything without explicit approval.

The goal is simple: make Codex show its work when it says a product experience needs improvement.
