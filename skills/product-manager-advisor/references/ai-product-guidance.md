# AI Product Guidance

Use this reference when the product involves model discovery, benchmarking, AI workflows, research tooling, agent systems, or model-building loops.

## Product Archetypes

Do not assume every AI-related project should become a startup or a monetized SaaS product. Some projects are better evaluated as:

- internal leverage tools
- editorial/intelligence assets
- credibility builders
- research infrastructure
- monetizable products

## AI Product Questions

For AI-heavy products, always ask:

- What is the durable value once the initial model novelty wears off?
- Is the moat data, workflow, trust, curation, distribution, speed, or proprietary insight?
- How much of the user value depends on third-party model providers?
- What breaks when the underlying models improve or become commoditized?
- Is the product actually a wrapper, or does it create compounding value over time?
- Are evals, sources, or taxonomies trustworthy enough for repeated use?
- What should be human-reviewed versus fully automated?

## Benchmark, Map, And Research Tool Guidance

For benchmarks, market maps, and research tooling, distinguish among multiple product possibilities:

- the benchmark or dataset itself
- the workflow around it
- the visibility or monitoring layer around it
- the editorial or intelligence product built on top

Evaluate:

- whether the product is optimizing for research progress, learning, content value, or commercial software
- what the minimum lovable workflow is
- where observability is missing
- whether local-first is a feature or a temporary shortcut
- what productionization would actually require: auth, job control, persistence, sharing, cost controls, security, and auditability

For dashboards and loop monitors, do not confuse "useful to the creator" with "ready for broader users."

## Recommendation Style

When advising on these products:

- be explicit about whether the recommendation is editorial, product, platform, or monetization advice
- identify the likely user archetype
- name the compounding asset being created, if any
- state whether the recommendation is prototype-grade or production-grade
- prefer the smallest experiment that reveals willingness to return, trust, or pay
