# Monetization

Treat monetization for AI Matrix Map as a product design question, not just a pricing exercise.

## Likely Stronger Paid Directions

More promising than "pay to browse the map":

- premium research workflows
- company comparison tools
- change alerts and watchlists
- structured export or API access
- curated intelligence summaries
- investor or operator research packages

## Less Promising Paid Directions

Be skeptical of:

- generic subscription with weak differentiation
- charging for basic directory access
- paywalling trust-building source detail that makes the public product credible
- premium plans with no repeated workflow

## Monetization Questions

Always ask:

- Who is paying: investor, corp strategy team, founder, recruiter, journalist, or researcher?
- What recurring decision does this help them make?
- Why would they come back every week or month?
- What part of the product gets more valuable over time?
- What premium layer can exist without hollowing out the free product?

## Good Tests

Prefer tests like:

- landing page for alerts or comparison workflows
- concierge research offer based on the same product angle
- waitlist for premium watchlists
- private beta for exports or workflow tools

Do not recommend pricing precision before the paid use case is clear.
