# Audit Heuristics

Use these as prompts for investigation, not as a substitute for evidence.

## Severity

- **Blocker:** prevents the primary goal or creates a serious incorrect or unsafe outcome.
- **High:** causes major confusion, abandonment, loss of trust, or a broken important path.
- **Medium:** creates repeatable friction or makes a meaningful task harder than necessary.
- **Low:** polish, consistency, or minor clarity issue with limited immediate impact.

## Questions to ask

- Can a first-time user tell what this product is, who it is for, and what to do next?
- Does every important action provide a clear result or next step?
- Can users recover from mistakes without losing work?
- Are loading, empty, success, and error states distinct and understandable?
- Does the interface explain why it needs information or permission?
- Are important controls visible, labeled, and reachable with the keyboard or touch?
- Does the product preserve user context across navigation, refresh, and return paths?
- Do the copy, visual hierarchy, and interaction model agree?
- Is the recommendation supported by an observed problem, or is it an opportunity hypothesis?
